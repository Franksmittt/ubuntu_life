What is Log Reduction?

In terms of infection control, ‘Log Reductions’ convey how effective a product is in reducing pathogens. The greater the log reduction the more effective the product is at killing bacteria and other pathogens that can cause infections. SANI-99™ for AGRI has a log reduction of 7-log which guarantees a pathogenic reduction of 99.99995%.

SANI-99™ for AGRI is a powerful agricultural disinfectant that is effective in preventing the spread and will suppress outbreaks of Newcastle disease (ND), Salmonellosis, E. coli, L. monocytogenes, Greasy Pig Disease caused by Staph, Swine Dysentery caused by
E. coli, Salmonella, Swine Flu, Foot and Mouth disease, Poxviridae, Avian Influenza virus (AIV) and many more.

Instructions for Use

Mix 1 x 96g SANI-99™ for AGRI disinfectant powder sachet with 16 litres of water as per diagram below. The following ratios must be adhered to for effective disinfection;

  • 96g = 16L to cover 160m2 highly contaminated area.
  • 96g = 16L to cover 400m2 medium contaminated area.
  • 96g = 16L to cover 800m2 low contaminated area.

Tailored Disinfectant Solutions

In the realm of agriculture, stringent biosecurity and disease prevention measures are crucial, particularly in sectors like poultry farming and abattoir operations, where the risk of diseases such as Avian Influenza (AI) and other contaminants can significantly impact production. SANI-99™ for AGRI has developed specialised disinfectant solutions catered to these critical areas of agriculture. These solutions are designed not only to combat the spread of diseases but also to maintain overall hygiene and safety in these environments.

Poultry Sector Solutions:

Avian Influenza, commonly known as bird flu, is a highly contagious viral disease with severe implications for poultry health, egg production, and economic stability in the farming industry. Moreover, the risk of transmission from birds to humans poses an additional public health concern. To effectively manage these risks, SANI-99™ for AGRI offers customised fogging solutions. This method involves dispersing a fine mist of disinfectant throughout the poultry house, ensuring comprehensive coverage. The technique is particularly effective in penetrating hard-to-reach areas that traditional cleaning methods often miss, providing an essential tool in the fight against AI.

Abattoir Disinfection Solutions:

Abattoirs require rigorous disinfection strategies to manage biological risks associated with the handling and processing of meat. SANI-99™ for AGRI extends its expertise to this sector by offering tailored disinfection protocols that include thorough cleaning and sanitisation processes. These protocols are designed to prevent the cross-contamination of pathogens between carcasses and ensure a safe environment for both the workforce and the end consumer. Regular disinfection through methods such as fogging and targeted spraying within abattoir environments helps maintain high standards of hygiene and compliance with health regulations.
