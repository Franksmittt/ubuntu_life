Makoko, a sprawling floating slum in Nigeria’s Lagos state, has long endured the harsh realities of inadequate infrastructure. Scarce electricity, insufficient waste disposal services, and a chronic lack of access to clean drinking water have plagued this former fishing community. The exact population of Makoko, is difficult to determine due to the nature of the community and limited data availability. Estimates range widely, with figures suggesting a population of around 40,000 to as much as 300,000 people.

The unregulated disposal of raw sewage and effluents into the surrounding waterways has led to dire consequences, resulting in highly contaminated and hazardous water conditions. However, in a remarkable turn of events, 2019 marked a significant milestone for the community as SANI AMANZI™, an innovative and groundbreaking water sanitiser, was introduced. This introduction sparked a newfound sense of hope and optimism, offering the community a tangible solution to address their long-standing water sanitation challenges.

The Challenge

The residents of Makoko faced formidable challenges concerning water quality and safety, posing significant risks to their well-being. The contamination levels in the local water sources escalated to alarming heights, rendering the water unfit for consumption. The absence of viable water treatment options further intensified the health concerns, leaving the community increasingly vulnerable to waterborne diseases and related health issues.​

Without access to clean and safe drinking water, the residents of Makoko faced a constant state of uncertainty and compromised health. Water-related illnesses became pervasive, with individuals experiencing ailments such as gastrointestinal infections, waterborne parasites, and bacterial diseases. The lack of proper water treatment infrastructure and limited resources for implementing effective sanitation measures intensified the gravity of the situation, leaving the community in a perpetual struggle for basic water sanitation needs.

In 2019, a watershed moment arrived for the Makoko community as SANI AMANZI™, a groundbreaking water sanitiser, was distributed throughout the region with the aim of addressing the urgent need for clean and safe drinking water.
